Larder Pro

Larder is free to use. Larder Pro adds the features that cost money to run.

£3.99a month
£29.99a year (save 37%)
  • Up to 10 AI recipes and 5 shopping photos a day (free: 3 AI recipes and 1 photo a week)
  • Shared kitchen with the people you live with
  • Shop prices and your shopping list's total
  • Dinner planner for the week

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged if you cancel before it ends. To go Pro, open Larder → Settings → Larder Pro.

Terms

  • Who you're buying from. Larder is run by an individual sole trader based in the UK. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.
  • Billing. After the free trial, your card is charged £3.99 every month or £29.99 every year, until you cancel. Prices include any taxes that apply. We'll tell you in advance if a price changes, and the new price only applies from your next renewal.
  • Cancelling. Cancel any time in Larder → Settings → Larder Pro → Manage subscription, or through the billing page. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for, then stops. Your food, lists and recipes stay on your phone.
  • Fair use. Daily AI limits keep costs sensible for everyone. AI suggestions (recipes, use-by dates, prices) are estimates. Always check food yourself before eating it, especially for allergies.
  • Pro codes. Your Pro code is for your own phones. Don't share it publicly.
  • Changes. We may improve, change or remove features. If we stop offering Pro, we'll refund any unused paid time.

Refunds

If you're charged and change your mind within 14 days, contact us for a full refund, no questions asked. After that, cancelling stops future payments but doesn't refund the current period. This doesn't affect your legal rights.

Privacy

  • On your phone. Your kitchen, shopping list, recipes and settings are stored on your phone.
  • Shared kitchens. If you share a kitchen, a copy of that kitchen (food, list, plan, saved recipes and preferences) is kept on Larder's server (Cloudflare) so the phones in your household stay in sync. Only people with your invite code can see it.
  • AI. When you use an AI feature, your request is sent to Google's Gemini service through Larder's server. That can include photos you take and the foods in your kitchen. Don't photograph anything personal.
  • Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details. We keep your subscription ID, Stripe customer ID and the email you gave at checkout, so Pro can be switched on and managed.
  • Reminders. If you turn on reminders, the server stores a push address and the dates you need reminding. It doesn't store the names of your foods.
  • No ads, no selling data. We don't use advertising trackers and we never sell your information.
  • Your rights. You can ask to see or delete what we hold about you by contacting us. Deleting the app removes what's on your phone.
